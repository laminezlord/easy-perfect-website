Infrastructure,start to finish.

A road or a bridge is only as good as the ground and the drainage under it. We are engineers first, so that part gets done properly before anyone sees the asphalt.

Bridges

Section B · How a road gets built

From surveyto surface.

  1. 01

    Survey & setting out

    Topographic survey, soil tests, and pegging out the alignment and levels.

  2. 02

    Design & costing

    Road, drainage and structural design with a priced bill of quantities, agreed before work starts.

  3. 03

    Construction

    Sub-base, stone base and asphalt laid and compacted layer by layer, with drains and kerbs built alongside.

  4. 04

    Handover

    Markings and streetlights in, tests passed, and as-built drawings handed over.
